The archive

Designed for reproducible solar analysis

The collection spans Carrington rotations CR2097–CR2312. Quantitative files are organized by wavelength and product, with separate uncertainty and quality-control resources where applicable.

Seven passbands

Extreme ultraviolet

94, 131, 171, 193, 211, 304, and 335 Å; baseline CEA intensity and three uncertainty FITS products per released rotation.

Two passbands

Ultraviolet

1600 and 1700 Å; baseline CEA maps, dimensionless CLV-relative maps, uncertainty and sampling diagnostics.

Data formats

Quantitative and visual

Individual FITS files provide numerical data. One 216-frame MP4 walkthrough per wavelength provides a visual check, not a replacement for quantitative FITS.

What is included

Scientific products and projections

Files are independent FITS products, not ZIP archives. The release selection prioritizes CEA representation for EUV and UV scientific maps; the published UV QC proposal includes CAR-coordinate diagnostic products.

Units: Baseline intensity and its intensity uncertainty are in DN/s. UV CLV-relative is dimensionless. Effective samples and time-offset diagnostics are not uncertainty standard deviations. Do not overlay CAR QC pixel arrays directly on CEA maps without reprojection.

Release notes

Quality and validation status

Verified transfer and inventory

  • 13 Harvard Dataverse draft datasets, totaling 9,954 files (9,932 FITS, nine MP4, 13 READMEs).
  • Remote checksums were checked during upload and release inventory verification.
  • All 9,932 selected local FITS passed file-presence inventory checks.
  • Harvard metadata descriptions and all 13 README files were checked after deployment.

Pending scientific sign-off

  • Pilot scientific validation reported 10 errors and six warnings across 226 sampled FITS; the issues need disposition.
  • AIA 131 Å CR2239 four science FITS withheld pending assessment.
  • Low-coverage rotations and WCS, CLV, and variance behavior require targeted review.
  • Checksums verify file identity, not scientific correctness or calibration.
i

No release approval implied. The repository website is public, but the Harvard datasets remain drafts until quality assessment, rights checks and publication are completed.
